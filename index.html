About

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematics at Georgia Institute of Technology, where my mentor is Michael Lacey. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in August 2025, where my advisor was Brian Street.

My current research interests are in discrete harmonic analysis, additive combinatorics, and quasilinear dispersive equations. Previously, my research focused on the study of multi-parameter singular integrals on graded Lie groups using tools from PDEs and Operator Algebra theory.
